Live Music

Four acts bringing soul, funk, blues, Americana and more to the Village Hall stage

Headline Act

Total Clusterfunk

A Bristol soul-funk 8-piece with a stellar show. Comprising former members of Roni Size and Reprazent, The Federation and Massive Attack, they bring pure energy to the stage — mixing brass-heavy funk classics, smooth soul gems and tight original tunes that are sure to get you moving.

"Blast your blues away with one of the best brass sections you'll hear all year!"Steve Henwood — Glastonbury Festivals & The Bell Inn, Bath
Find them on Facebook

SP3 Blues Trio

Legendary Bristol bluesman Steve Payne and guitarist Lyndon Webb, together with Yardbirds and Nine Below Zero harp man Billyboy Miskimmin — world class blues and boogie from three of the UK's finest exponents.

Visit their website

The Shrinks

England's premier exponents of 'Avonicana' — where the mighty Mississippi meets the muddy Avon. More Cotham than Country, The Shrinks offer an off-kilter cocktail of original songs and Americana covers played with vim.

Follow on X

Hester Miller

A regular at open mic nights around Bristol, the Malago-go Music Club and Victoria Park Festival. Hester brings a fabulous voice, wonderful ukulele playing and a warmth that fills any room.

Supporting Our Community

The Long Ashton Beer & Cider Festival has been running since 2013, and all proceeds go towards the Long Ashton Community Association — the charitable body that maintains and develops the Village Hall and the wide range of services it provides to the local community.

When you buy a ticket, you're not just signing up for a great evening — you're helping keep the heart of Long Ashton beating. Thank you.
